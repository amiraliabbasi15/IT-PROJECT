Warehouse Inventory Dashboard

Find an industrial spare part, see how many are left and spot what needs to be reordered.

Item list

Type in the box to filter the table. Rows in yellow have less stock than their minimum level.

Charts

Item types per category

Each bar counts how many different items a category contains. It shows how the catalogue is spread across categories.

Units in stock per category

Each bar adds up the stock of every item in the category. It shows where most of the physical inventory is stored.

Low-stock items per category

An item is low on stock when its stock is below its minimum level. The taller the bar, the more items in that category need a new purchase order soon.

Data source: simulated dataset for a university IT course. Part names follow common industrial standards; supplier names, stock levels and shelf locations are randomly generated.
